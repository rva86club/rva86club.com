8/6 Day

Join the Mid-Atlantic 86 Club, 86Philly, 86Raleigh, Richmond 86 Club, and Seven Cities 86 for a joint club overnight hangout. The clubs will cruise to Buc-ee’s in Mount Crawford, VA Saturday afternoon, stay the night at TRU by Hilton nearby, then have breakfast together Sunday morning before checking out local roads and scenery on the way home.
